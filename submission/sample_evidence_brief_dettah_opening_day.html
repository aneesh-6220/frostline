5. Road-operation evidence

The government record supports a change in the Dettah opening date over 1993/94 to 2024/25: +9.0 days per decade (later), 95% interval 3.6 to 12.4, q = 0.002 across 51 route-level tests. Over the shorter 2001/02 to 2024/25 window the estimate is +10.3 days per decade (interval 3.3 to 17.2) and is not supported after correction (q = 0.085), so the result is sensitive to the period. The NASA reanalysis freezing accumulation for the same cell shows +15 °C-days per decade (interval -108 to 111) over 2001/02 to 2024/25: not statistically distinguishable from no change, which is not proof of no change. 3 leak-free associations between NASA climate predictors and Dettah road dates were tested; every interval includes zero. Station observations disagree materially with the reanalysis in absolute terms (NASA freezing degree-days +46.5% against Yellowknife A), while tracking its year-to-year variation. The evidence does not show what produced the change in operating dates. Local ice, snow and operational records would be needed to investigate that.

Opening and closing dates are government records of operations, not measurements of ice thickness. The interval is not usable days.

6. What the evidence shows, layer by layer

A. Observations

  • Opening and closing dates of the Dettah Ice Road, one pair per winter, as published in NWT Bureau of Statistics, Table 108.
  • 32 verified winters in 1993/94 to 2024/25.

B. Derived indicators

  • Road opening day: Recorded opening date of the road, counted in days since Oct 1 of the winter. A higher number is a later opening.

C. Statistical inference

  • Across 32 winters (1993/94 to 2024/25), the estimated change in opening day at Dettah is +9.0 days per decade (later), with a 95% interval of 3.6 to 12.4.
  • Block-bootstrap 95% interval 5.2 to 12.6; Mann-Kendall p = <0.001 (unadjusted).
  • q = 0.002 within all 51 road-date tests across 17 NWT routes (winters 1993/94 to 2024/25).
  • Evidence of a monotonic change in this record (q = 0.002 within the stated family). Exploratory; this does not show what caused it.

D. External corroboration

  • No independent record of opening and closing dates is held in the app data.
  • Published reports were used to spot-check route identity and some dates (documented in docs/ROUTE_IDENTITY.md).
  • NASA Oct to Dec freezing degree-days for the same cell (winters 2001/02 to 2024/25): +15 °C-days per decade, 95% interval -108 to 111. Not statistically distinguishable from no change in this record. That is not proof of no change.

E. Limitations

  • A government record of operations, not a measurement of ice thickness or road condition.
  • Opening and closing reflect construction, ice surveys, snow, maintenance and administrative decisions as well as weather.
  • Temporary closures are not recorded.
  • Dettah was chosen as the primary case after its road-date trend had been seen, so its road-date results are selection-biased. The all-route family of 51 tests is the adjustment.
  • Nothing here is a statement of cause, a road-safety statement or a prediction.
  • No statistician has signed off on these methods; the analysis is exploratory.

F. Unanswered questions

  • What do local ice-thickness and snow measurements show for these winters? None are in this dataset.
  • How did construction timing, equipment and operating rules change over the record? Operational history is not recorded here.
  • Is the later-opening estimate robust to the earliest winters? Dropping the first five winters of 2001 to 2024 leaves an interval that touches zero.
  • Has an independent statistician reviewed the methods? Sign-off is pending.
