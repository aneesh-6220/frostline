Understand the changing northern winter

Frostline lets you investigate historical winter conditions at two Northwest Territories ice-road settings using NASA POWER daily reanalysis (MERRA-2), ECCC station records and NWT Bureau of Statistics road-operations records. Every number is computed from the committed per-winter data with the project's approved methods.

What Frostline can and cannot tell you

  • It reports historical trends with 95% intervals, sample sizes and an evidence state. A result that is not resolved is reported as such.
  • It shows how gridded NASA temperatures disagree with the nearby ground station, so you can judge how far to trust them.
  • It does not forecast anything and does not assess road safety. Road dates are records of operations, not ice measurements.
