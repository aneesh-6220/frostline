# Research evidence (context, with claim limits)

Full bibliographic details and verification status are in `REFERENCES.md`. This file says what the literature supports and what Frostline must not claim from it.

## What the literature supports
- Winter roads are an important seasonal transport mode in northern Canada: about 10,000 km nationally (TAC paper), the most economical way to move supplies to many remote communities (ClimateData.ca), described as a critical seasonal lifeline for remote First Nation communities in Ontario's Far North (Hori et al. 2018, Regional Environmental Change).
- The James Bay Winter Road study (Hori et al. 2017) found significantly decreasing FDD over 1961-2014 but no statistically significant link between opening dates and FDD, and a lowest viable Oct-Dec FDD of 380 degree-days for that road. It also found Oct-Dec FDD more closely linked to opening than FDD accumulated to the opening date. That second point is about preconditioning; it does not transfer to the NWT.
- For the Tibbitt to Contwoyto Winter Road, Song et al. (2026) use FDD for opening and downward thermal radiation for closing, and Mullan et al. (2021) use a process-based lake-ice model. The physical quantity that governs opening is ice thickness, which air temperature only influences.

## What it does not support (Frostline must not claim)
- That the 380 C-day threshold applies to Dettah, Aklavik or any NWT road, or to NASA POWER FDD (which is biased cold at Yellowknife; `TEMPERATURE_BIAS_DIAGNOSIS.md`).
- That Frostline is the first analysis of freezing accumulation and winter-road opening dates (Hori et al. did this for James Bay). The honest statement is that Frostline applies a transparent, reproducible trend-detection workflow with leakage and multiplicity controls to NWT road records.
- That any trend found here is caused by climate change. The road record is an operational record; ice thickness, snow, overflow, construction timing and decisions all affect it.

## Dettah context (secondary sources, `ROUTE_IDENTITY.md`)
The Dettah ice road is a short lake crossing with an all-season alternative, opens to light vehicles first after about 34 to 36 cm of ice, and has had late openings attributed in press reports to snow insulation and overflow. Do not call it a lifeline.

## What existing tools offer
ClimateData.ca provides climate data, indices and projections for Canada. A short search did not find a public tool combining NASA daily temperature trends, significance, NWT opening and closing records and uncertainty; this was not an exhaustive review, so use "to our knowledge" if at all.

## What Frostline can and cannot determine
Can: describe trends and their uncertainty in NASA reanalysis series and in NWT road dates, and report associations with leak-free predictors, all labelled exploratory. Cannot: ice thickness or safety; future opening dates; the cause of a road-date change; usable days; conditions at every point on a road; results for other roads.
